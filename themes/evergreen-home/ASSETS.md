# Original artwork and provenance

Six artwork generations used the built-in image_gen tool, with the user’s board as a composition reference only. No pixels from that board are shipped. Exact final prompts and original PNG paths are in `generation-prompts.json`. Final runtime files are in `assets/` inside this theme; original sources remain in the recorded generated-images folder.

| Asset | Preparation |
|---|---|
| welcome.webp | Original portrait modern villa; 600×900 WebP quality 74 |
| house-wide.webp | Independent whole landscape villa; 760×507, quality 74 |
| house-desktop.webp | Landscape welcome, 1240×827, quality 72; desktop-only source |
| living.webp / dining.webp | Two upper quadrants of the interiors source, 500px wide, quality 76 |
| puja.webp / refreshments.webp | Lower quadrants of the interiors source, 360px wide, quality 76 |

| terrace-desktop.webp | Independent landscape night terrace, 1240×827, quality 75; desktop-only source |
| sample/* | Copied shared placeholders and original generated repository music drone |

Interior source extraction bounds: living (0,0,750,492), dining (786,0,1536,492), puja (0,526,750,1024), refreshments (786,526,1536,1024). White gutters are excluded. No AI-generated lettering is included. All artwork depicts fictional property; no real client, celebrity or private person is represented. Edited derivatives are crops/resizes/compression only. No fabricated guest testimonials are shown.

Authored inline SVGs provide house, coffee, tableware, people, faith and navigation symbols. Google Fonts Bodoni Moda, Source Sans 3 and Allura are OFL families; they are the only font service. Allura is limited to fixed English decorative text. Live names use the display serif with Indic-safe CSS. The local shared QR encoder is the repository’s MIT component. All imagery and audio stay local; Google Maps and tap-activated YouTube are the allowed external embeds.
